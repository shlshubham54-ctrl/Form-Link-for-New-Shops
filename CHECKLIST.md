# Sign-up form ("Connect with sdhiryan")

Live at https://form-link-for-new-shops.vercel.app/ (re-upload this `onboarding/` folder, including `upi-qr.png`, after changes).
The shopkeeper fills it in and taps **Send on WhatsApp**. A formatted message opens to +91 85330 53912.
Then, back on the form, **Send photos to sdhiryan** shares all their photos in one go (Android / iPhone share sheet).

## What it asks
1. **What do you need?** (required, multiple choice): New tap page · Extra NFC cards / stands · Replace damaged card · Renew my plan · Update my page
2. **Your shop:** shop name*, type of shop, owner name*, phone number*, address*, location (one-tap GPS or a pasted Google Maps link)
3. **Online:** Instagram, YouTube
4. **Photos:** logo, banner / poster, menu card (all pages)
5. **What should your page have?** Menu · Google review · Instagram & YouTube · Directions · Payment QR → review · NFC review card · Rewards card · Wi‑Fi · Anonymous feedback · Mini game
6. **Advance payment:** your UPI QR (dhiryansd999@okicici), Copy UPI ID, Pay with UPI app, Save QR, status (Paid / Will pay later), payment screenshot

The form remembers answers, photos and scroll position if they leave and come back.
`?ref=<shop>` (added by every shop page's "Created by" button) shows up as "Saw it at: …" in the message.
